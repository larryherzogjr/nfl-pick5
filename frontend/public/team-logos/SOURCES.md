# NFL team logos

Retrieved September 6, 2026 from the team logo URLs in ESPN's public NFL directory:
https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/teams

Each PNG's source is `https://a.espncdn.com/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/{abbr}.png`,
where `{abbr}` is the lowercase local filename without its extension.
The directory's 32 abbreviations match the application's backend team mapping.

Served locally to avoid third-party requests and external availability delays.
These are team identification graphics; ownership remains with the respective
rights holders. No ownership or redistribution license is asserted by this app.
